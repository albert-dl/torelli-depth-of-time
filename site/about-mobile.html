about us

Torelli Furniture is fast becoming one of the UK's leading suppliers of modern and contemporary furniture. We offer a wide range of luxury home and garden furniture.

our aim

Our aim is to provide our customers with vast choice of All Your Modern Designer Furniture Needs Under One Roof. In order to do this we design, source, stock and deliver the highest quality of modern furniture sourced from around the world. We have a workforce which is super-passionate about supplying the very best in luxury furniture and this is why we are constantly developing our products and services to super seed the aspirations of our discerning consumers.

why torelli?

We believe we are better than our competitors:

 

unique products

We design and source many of our products from exclusive manufacturers who continually lead the market in elegance, style and fashion.

 

product quality

Our products are strictly checked during the manufacturing process and QC'd once the production is complete and before goods are packed in our superior packaging.

 

in stock

We hold stock of more than 90% of our items at our huge 40,000Sqft warehouses – this means we can deliver your order when you need it.

 

reliable delivery service

We have a large network of delivery teams that cover the UK to deliver your order safely and professionally.

 

great customer service

We are committed to providing the highest level of service to our customer. From providing the most up-to-date stock information to advising on product aftercare.

 

value for money

Buying a Torelli product will represent excellent value for money, yet it will look as though this luxury has cost a fortune.
